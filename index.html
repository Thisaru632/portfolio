The Journey

Aria Sterling discovered her passion for performing on the stages of London before transitioning into the world of high-fashion modeling in Paris. Her unique ability to convey complex emotions through both movement and dialogue has made her a sought-after talent for brands and directors alike.

With over 5 years of experience in the industry, she has walked for major fashion houses and appeared in critically acclaimed independent films. She is currently based in New York City.
